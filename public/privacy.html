Privacy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

OfferGrid is a price-discovery and affiliate-comparison service. This page explains the basic information the site may process while it operates.

Server and security logs

The server and infrastructure providers may process technical request information such as IP addresses, timestamps, browser/user-agent data, requested URLs, and security events. OfferGrid may use this information for reliability, fraud prevention, debugging, and abuse prevention.

Anonymous visitor counts

OfferGrid may store a random browser identifier in local storage so the site can estimate currently connected, historically unique, and returning browser users. The identifier is hashed on the server before it is recorded; OfferGrid does not use this counter as a customer account or store a name or email with it. OfferGrid may associate coarse visit timestamps with that hash to estimate rolling 30-day returning-visitor metrics. While a public OfferGrid page is open, the browser sends a lightweight presence heartbeat about every five minutes so active-user and visit-session estimates can stay current.

When enabled at OfferGrid’s network edge, the private Admin dashboard may also receive an approximate city, region, country, latitude, and longitude inferred by the infrastructure provider from a visitor’s connection. OfferGrid rounds coordinates to two decimal places before storage, does not place the raw IP address in its visitor-location history, and automatically limits that approximate location history to 30 days by default. This is not browser GPS or precise device location.

Affiliate links and third parties

When you follow a merchant link, OfferGrid may record an outbound click and redirect you to a merchant or affiliate-network URL. Affiliate providers, merchants, Squirrel, advertising providers, and other third parties may use their own cookies or tracking technologies under their own privacy policies.

Campaign links may include source, medium, campaign, placement, and Dallas-area labels. OfferGrid stores those labels in signed, HTTP-only first-party cookies so a visit and later outbound merchant click can be attributed to the campaign. The visitor cookie lasts up to one year, campaign attribution lasts up to 90 days by default, and a visit session lasts 30 minutes by default. OfferGrid sends a random per-click reference to supported affiliate networks so reported commissions can be reconciled without sending a name, email address, or raw IP address to the network as that reference.

Commission reporting

OfferGrid may import transaction and commission reports from its affiliate partners. Those records can include a transaction ID, network, merchant, date, status, order amount, commission amount, campaign labels, and the anonymous click reference supplied with the affiliate link. These records are used for campaign measurement, accounting, fraud review, and commission reconciliation.

Admin authentication

The private Admin dashboard uses an HTTP-only authentication cookie. It is not intended for customer accounts and is used only to protect site administration.

Price data

OfferGrid stores product, offer, merchant, and observed price-history data used to generate comparisons and Deal Heat. Product data may originate from connected APIs or affiliate sources.

Contact

Privacy or security questions can be sent to api@offergrid.tech.

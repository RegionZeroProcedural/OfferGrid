Coverage and lowest-price claims

OfferGrid compares offers from supported sources that it has actually discovered, tracked, or verified. Unless expressly stated otherwise, labels such as “lowest tracked offer,” “best tracked offer,” or similar rankings are scoped to the offers currently known to OfferGrid. They do not mean that every seller, coupon, local price, personalized promotion, or purchasing option has been found, and a lower price may exist elsewhere.

Affiliate compensation does not make OfferGrid's coverage comprehensive.
